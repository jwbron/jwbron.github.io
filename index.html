What I can do for your team

Guardrails and safe connectors

Give agents access to your tools, integrations and data without handing them the keys. I build the harness around the agent and the connectors it uses: sandboxes, brokered credentials and scoped permissions, with people approving the calls that matter. I've worked extensively with sensitive student data, so confidentiality is the starting point, not an afterthought.

Evals and cost

Repeatable tests of how agents and models actually perform, with cost tracked per model. I bring spend down by making usage more efficient, not by rationing it.

Review agents that help

Agents that check work before anyone relies on it, whether that's a contract, a research memo, a report or code. Findings get verified before they reach a person, follow-ups only look at what changed, and the people using it shape how it works.

Adoption and training

Tools only pay off if people use them. I teach teams, engineers and non-engineers alike, to work well with AI, set practical best practices, and keep the tools simple enough that they stick.

Faster, cheaper delivery

CI, deployment pipelines and cloud platforms that are quick, cheap to run and easy for your team to own after I'm gone.

Working with me

6,237 ft
  1. I start with how your team works today. Before changing anything, I learn the existing workflow from the people in it.
  2. Then I pick one bounded problem. A review workflow, an agent setup, or a slow and expensive system. Something with a clear before and after, and a way to measure it.
  3. I build it with the team, not for them. Their feedback shapes it, and I own the rollout and the rough edges, not just the first version. Limits live in the system itself, not in instructions an agent might ignore.
  4. I leave something you can run. Reusable infrastructure and a real handoff, so it keeps working after I'm gone.
