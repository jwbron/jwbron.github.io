What I can do for your team

Guardrails and safe connectors

Give agents access to your tools, integrations and data through connections they can't misuse. They only get the access a task needs, and a person approves the decisions that matter.

I've spent years working with sensitive student, health and financial data.

Evaluations and cost

Repeatable tests of how agents and models actually perform, with cost tracked per model. Spend is broken down by model, role and phase, so you can see what each workflow costs.

Review agents that help

Agents that check work before anyone relies on it, whether that's a contract, a research memo, a report or code. Findings are checked against the source before they reach a person, follow-ups only look at what changed, and the people using it shape how it works.

Adoption and training

I teach teams, engineers and non-engineers alike, to work well with AI, set practical best practices, and keep the tools simple enough that they stick.

Faster, cheaper delivery

Build, test and deployment pipelines and cloud platforms that are quick, cheap to run and easy for your team to own. My self-hosted CI runners at Khan Academy cut compute costs in half.

Working with me

6,237 ft
  1. I start with how your team works today. Before changing anything, I learn the existing workflow from the people in it.
  2. Then I pick one bounded problem. A review workflow, an agent setup, or a slow and expensive system. Something with a clear before and after, and a way to measure it.
  3. I build it with the team. Their feedback shapes it, and I own the rollout and the rough edges along with the first version.
  4. I leave something you can run. Reusable infrastructure and a real handoff, so it keeps working after I'm gone.
