What I can do for your team

Evals

Working with me

6,237 ft
  1. I start with how your team works today. Before changing anything, I learn the existing workflow from the people in it.
  2. Then I pick one bounded problem. A review pipeline, an agent setup, or a slow and expensive CI system. Something with a clear before and after.
  3. I build it with the team, not for them. Their feedback shapes it, and I own the rollout and the rough edges, not just the first version.
  4. I leave something you can run. Reusable infrastructure and a real handoff, so it keeps working after I'm gone.

Two habits come with me everywhere. If an agent mustn't do something, I remove the capability instead of writing a rule. And I want evidence, not agreement. I once watched eight out of eight AI reviewers approve code that didn't build.
